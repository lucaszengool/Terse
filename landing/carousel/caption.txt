Compressing an LLM prompt by 60% — in under 50ms, on a laptop, with no model call.

Most "prompt optimizers" just ask GPT to rewrite your prompt. That's slow, it spends tokens to save tokens, and it ships your data to a server.

We wanted something local, instant, and private. Here's how Terse does it 👇 (swipe)

→ Tokenize the way the model does — real BPE boundaries, not character count
→ Strip zero-signal filler (politeness, hedging) — ~15–25% with identical output
→ Leave clean technical prompts at 0%; over-compression is worse than none
→ Catch the real waste: the same file read 3×, a 40K-token diff for 12 lines

40–70% off verbose input, 0% on clean input, all on-device.

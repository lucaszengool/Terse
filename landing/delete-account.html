Delete your Terse account

This permanently deletes your Terse account and the data stored with it, on every device. It can't be undone.
删除 Terse 账号：永久删除账号及其在服务器上的数据，所有设备同时生效，无法恢复。

What gets deleted

  • Your account, sign-in and profile
  • Plaza posts, comments, likes and reports — and comments others left on your posts
  • Direct messages you sent or received
  • Room memberships and your chat messages; rooms you created are closed
  • Friends, friend links, linked computers, wallpapers and notification subscriptions

What happens to billing

  • A subscription bought on our website (Stripe) is cancelled immediately.
  • A subscription bought in Google Play or the App Store must be cancelled there — Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Payment records kept by Stripe, Google or Apple are retained by them as the law requires. Server logs and backups roll off within 30 days.
